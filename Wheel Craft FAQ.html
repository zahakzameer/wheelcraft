Questions

Frequently asked

What kind of damage can you repair? +

Kerb rash, gutter damage, corrosion, scratches, bends, cracks and structural faults across alloy and some steel wheels — send photos for an accurate quote.

How long does a repair take? +

Most cosmetic repairs are completed in 2–4 business days. Structural repairs, custom colour work or diamond cutting can take 5–7 days.

Are crack repairs road-legal? +

We assess every crack against current VicRoads structural repair regulations. Some cracks are repairable, others require replacement — we will always tell you which.

What warranty do you offer? +

Every finish we apply is backed by a 2-year written warranty against workmanship defects.

Can you match my exact wheel colour? +

Yes — we colour match to OE codes or a custom sample, and can spray a test panel first on request.

Do you offer pickup and delivery? +

Yes, free pickup and delivery is available for trade accounts, and can be arranged for retail customers on request.

Can chrome wheels be re-plated? +

True chrome re-plating isn't offered in-house, but we do offer a chrome-shadow finish that replicates the look on a standard alloy.

Do you repair steel or motorcycle wheels? +

We repair select steel wheels and most motorcycle alloy wheels — get in touch with photos and we will confirm.
